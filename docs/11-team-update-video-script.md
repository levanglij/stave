# 11 — Team Update Video Script (~2 min)

> Format: founder on camera, intercut with short screen recordings of the live site. Loom or Twitter/X. **Not** the formal pitch video (`04-pitch-video-script.md`) or the tech demo (`05-tech-demo-script.md`) — this is a build-in-public status update for the last week of the Solana Frontier Hackathon 2026. Tone: confident, concrete, time-honest. Acknowledges late entry and uses it as proof of velocity rather than as an apology.

## Length budget

~280 words spoken at a confident pace = ~120 seconds. Target 1:55–2:05.

---

## Beats

### 0:00 – 0:12 · Hook *(on camera)*

> "Hey — I'm **Levan**, financial director at **Stave**. Quick update on where we are with the **Solana Frontier Hackathon**. We came in late, but we've shipped a lot in a short window, and I want to walk you through what's done and what's still to come."

*~28 words · 12 sec.*

> "Stave is the **rating-and-financing infrastructure for music royalty assets on Solana** — a Moody's of music. Catalogs get tokenized into fractional shares, investors buy them, royalties flow back pro-rata. Every listing ships with a quantitative credit rating from a five-layer engine."

*~44 words · 22 sec.*

That's the moat: ground-truth ownership data, not modeled streaming counts. No pure-tech competitor can replicate this access."

*~50 words · 20 sec.*

### 1:30 – 1:50 · What's next *(on camera)*

*~32 words · 10 sec.*

## Production notes

- **One take preferred over editing.** This is a status update; polish reads as overproduction.
- **Background:** neutral. Office, white wall, or branded backdrop. No clutter.
- **Audio:** clean mic (Lavalier or USB condenser). No music underbed — a status update wants to feel direct, not produced.
- **Captions burned in** for muted autoplay on Twitter/X.
- **B-roll cuts** during 0:35–1:10 only. Rest of the video is on-camera.
- **Lower thirds** if used: keep to "Levan Gvarishvili · Financial Director · Stave" and "stave.cc" + "github.com/levanglij/stave".

## Delivery

- Loom: link directly.
- Twitter/X: ≤2:20 max for native upload; this script lands at ~2:00. Caption: short hook + repo URL + live URL + #SolanaFrontier.
- LinkedIn: same video; longer post text framing the institutional angle (Moody's of music + IPOA).
- Colosseum / Discord: post in the project channel as a status update.

## Variant (if you want a 60-sec cut)

Drop beats 4 (data moat) and trim beat 5 (next steps) to a single sentence:

> "Last-week sprint: deploying to Solana devnet, recording the pitch and tech-demo videos, finalising the deck. Post-hackathon plan including VASP licensing is in the repo."

That brings the total to ~190 words / ~80 seconds, which fits a Twitter/X post tightly.

## Ask the dev to watch this and timebox

Before recording, ask the dev (and one other team member if available) to watch a draft and flag any technical claim that needs softening. The on-chain program lines (`five MVP instructions and fifteen passing tests`) are precise — don't round them up if the deploy hasn't happened by the time you record.
