## ✅ Confirmed (from Colosseum submission-guidance blog — applies across all Colosseum hackathons)

### Required deliverables (per submission-guidance blog)

1. **Product name**
2. **Short product description**
3. **Team background**
4. **GitHub repository** — public, OR closed-source with private access granted to Colosseum
5. **Pitch video** (≤ 3 minutes)
6. **Technical walkthrough video** (≤ 3 minutes — "a new addition to this year's hackathon")
7. **Anything else critical to understanding the vision**

### Pitch video specs

- **Max duration: 3 minutes.** Exceeding this is listed as a common reason submissions are downgraded.
- Must include: team background, problem being solved, who the product is for, any validation/feedback received, broader vision
- "Treat their pitch like a brief startup pitch, not a product demo"
- "The pitch video is the most important element of the submission. It is usually the first item judges review."

### Tech demo video specs

- **Duration: 2–3 minutes** (under 3 min)
- Walk through core features built, explain tech stack, decisions on prioritization
- "About the how" rather than the why

### Common rejection / downgrade reasons (per submission-guidance blog)

- Exceeding the 3-minute pitch-video limit
- Overly flashy visuals with little substance
- Over-relying on buzzwords
- Vague or overly technical descriptions
- Omitting team background
- Submitting incomplete or unpolished pitch videos
- Failing to explain Solana integration clearly
- Ignoring optional fields that could provide important context
- **Forgetting to grant judges access to Google Docs, pitch videos, GitHub repos, etc.**

## 🟡 Likely (inferred from Renaissance 2024 official rules — Frontier 2026 wording may differ)

The Renaissance 2024 PDF is the closest publicly readable Colosseum rules document. The following items typically carry forward across cycles, but **are not confirmed for Frontier 2026** and should be verified against the Frontier rules PDF before submission day.

### Eligibility (Renaissance 2024 wording)

- Age of majority in country of residence, OR 18+, whichever is older. Younger participants may email `hello@colosseum.org` for case-by-case consideration.
- **Excluded jurisdictions:** Afghanistan, Belarus, Cuba, Iran, North Korea, Russia, Somalia, Syria, the Crimea/Sevastopol/Donetsk/Luhansk/Zaporizhzhia/Kherson regions of Ukraine, Venezuela, Yemen.
- **Excluded individuals:** anyone on US/UN/EU sanctions lists (OFAC, Entity List, etc.); employees/contractors/officers of Colosseum, Solana Foundation, sponsors, and their affiliates and Immediate Family.
- Participation must not violate any third-party rights or employer policies.

### Limits

- Each Individual may be a Member of **only one Team**.
- Each Team may submit **only one Project Submission** at a time.

### Judging criteria (Renaissance 2024 — Frontier wording UNCERTAIN, see "Uncertain" section below)

Renaissance 2024 listed six criteria, no published weights:
- (a) **Functionality:** how well does it work; code quality
- (b) **Potential impact:** TAM; impact on the broader Solana ecosystem
- (c) **Novelty:** how unique is the concept
- (d) **UX:** how the project utilizes Solana's performance for downstream UX
- (e) **Open-source:** is it open-source; how well it composes with other Solana primitives
- (f) **Business plan:** is there a viable future business

**Frontier 2026 may have adjusted criteria** — the Frontier announcement says "the most impactful product submission" without enumerating sub-criteria.

### Content guidelines (Renaissance 2024)

- **All Content must be in English.**
- Must not infringe IP, violate privacy/publicity rights, contain malicious code, be defamatory, or promote bigotry/hatred/discrimination.
- Must not violate the terms of any third-party platform where content is uploaded.

### Disqualification triggers (Renaissance 2024)

- Failure to provide registration profile information or express consent
- Profile information not conforming to the content guidelines
- Tampering with the contest, unsportsmanlike or disruptive behavior
- Content not in English
- Content violating any of the content restrictions above
- Sponsorship by an unapproved third party

### IP / ownership (Renaissance 2024)

- Entrant retains all IP in the Project Submission. Administrator does not claim ownership.
- Administrator retains rights to "Creative Materials" — promotional materials it produces about the contest, which may include the Entrant's name, image, likeness, and Content.
- Entrants must inform Administrator of any open-source or third-party code in the submission.

### Disputes (Renaissance 2024)

- ICC arbitration, single arbitrator, English, State of Florida governing law.
- No class actions; damages limited to actual out-of-pocket costs.
